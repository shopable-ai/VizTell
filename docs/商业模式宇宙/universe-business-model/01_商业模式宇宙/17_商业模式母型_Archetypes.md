# 商业模式母型 Archetypes

本文件保存市场中具有稳定认知的典型商业模式组合。母型不是商业模式宇宙本身，而是宇宙中常见的成熟组合。

## 产品与资产型
- 产品销售
- DTC
- Razor and Blades
- 租赁
- 分时与共享
- 再制造与循环经济

## 服务型
- 专业服务
- 咨询
- Managed Service
- Productized Service
- 外包
- Outcome-based Service

## 软件与数字产品型
- SaaS
- Usage-based Software
- API Business
- Open-core
- Freemium Software
- Enterprise Software Licensing

## 平台与交易型
- Marketplace
- Managed Marketplace
- Multi-sided Platform
- Aggregator
- Broker
- Exchange
- App Marketplace

## 注意力、内容与社区型
- Advertising-supported
- Sponsorship
- Creator Economy
- Membership Community
- Subscription Media
- UGC Platform

## IP、数据与技术型
- Licensing
- Franchise
- Data-as-a-Product
- Insights-as-a-Service
- Technology Licensing
- White Label / OEM

## 金融型
- Lending
- Payments
- Insurance
- Brokerage
- Float-based
- Embedded Finance

## 生态与基础设施型
- Infrastructure-as-a-Service
- Ecosystem Platform
- Developer Platform
- Standard / Certification
- Embedded Distribution

## 混合型
现实企业经常同时组合产品、订阅、服务、金融、广告、数据、平台与生态收入。
